Toolkit for Liquid Budget

Transactions

Adds a 4px border where the transaction list crosses a month boundary (e.g. Jan 31 → Feb 1).

Left menu

Tightens the rows from Budget to Recurring Transactions (32px rows, 2px apart instead of 36px and 4px).

Gives error bars above the budget (Budget Overdraft and others) a light red band, a red edge and larger text.

Hides the Net Worth card at the bottom of the left sidebar.

Shows amounts without the $ (or your budget's currency symbol) everywhere in Liquid Budget.

Budget screen

For the current month, each bucket's Available has transactions dated later this month taken out, with a calendar-clock icon listing them. You can also switch this with the calendar-clock button next to the AVAILABLE column header.

Adds a line per coming month (up to 12 ahead) that already has money assigned, with that month's total, under All Money Assigned.

On Reports → Overview for the current month, extends the Inflow and Outflow bars with a lighter, striped segment for transactions dated later this month (scheduled and pending), and updates Monthly net and Liquid budget to match.

Adds a Cash Flow tab after Income vs Expense: a Sankey chart of the month from income sources through your budget to categories and buckets, with pending and scheduled amounts striped.

Right panel

Hover a card's header (All Money Assigned, Days of Buffer, Overview, History…) and drag the grip at its left to move it, or focus the grip and use the up and down arrow keys. The order is kept separately for the budget view and for a selected bucket.

Puts the cards back in Liquid Budget's own order.

Days of Buffer

A widget below All Money Assigned: how many days your account group's balance would last at the last 90 days' spending.

The group's name exactly as it appears in the left sidebar (for example, Liquid).

Also changeable from the cog on the widget.

Takes what you owe on credit cards off the balance before dividing (also in the widget's cog menu).

Retirement Horizon

A widget below Days of Buffer: how close your invested money is to financial independence. It works out your FI number (a year of spending ÷ your withdrawal rate, 25× at 4%), what a safe withdrawal pays a month and a year, and how many years until you get there with your current saving and a yearly growth rate after inflation. The withdrawal rate, growth, savings outside the budget, which account groups count and which categories count as spending are all set from the widget's cog.

Pacing

Adds a Pacing page under Projections: how the current month is going, counting every transaction dated this month (pending and scheduled ones included), which buckets are projected to run out of money, and quick assign amounts that are out of step with what you actually spend.

Pacing reads your budget through Liquid Budget's Developer API. Create a read-only token (lbro_) under Developer → API Tokens. The token stays in this browser's extension storage and is only sent to liquidbudget.com.
